# Training Plan, weekly plans and KPIs

Training Plan opens to **Current week** on your device's Monday–Sunday calendar. **Next week** includes plans prepared on Sunday. **General Plan** is an independent manual editor; a plan older than 60 elapsed days can prompt optional review, not expiry/rewrite. **History** loads bounded summaries and individual documents, not the whole archive.

Create/revise plans and reviews directly in chat with current Garmin evidence and feedback; no mandatory preview/confirmation turn is required. Say **draft only, do not save** for discussion without persistence. Complete reads, conflict/privacy/calendar checks and exact edits still apply. Review plus next plan can save atomically; General Plan is separate. The trainer reports each actual success/failure. [Coaching policy](coaching-instructions-and-sources.md#weekly-planning-and-review) owns write intent, evidence and suggestions.

## Saved targets and notes

The compact **KPI | Target | Actual | Points** grid uses the latest established plan and one total. Each scored numeric measurement has its own points; monitoring is unscored. Necessary qualifications use row disclosures. [Weekly KPI contracts](weekly-kpis.md) own identities, optional targets/points, 100-point agreement, explained extra/partial credit, provisional automatic arithmetic and exact assessment references. This is plan achievement, not fitness improvement. No recorded plan means no completion score; missing evidence is unavailable, not zero. Superseded targets are not alternative baselines.

Current/Next Plan notes open at full grid width. History shows Review before Plan, both closed; older documents/assessments remain stored. Reopening refreshes saved data and returns to Current week; loaded History remains available. Navigation/successful saves refresh reads, with Retry only on failure, not routine Reload. Entering General Plan refreshes its saved snapshot while preserving unsaved editor input; Cancel restores the current saved snapshot. Navigation never saves drafts.

Historical commitments are read-only. Sunday is not automatically complete; late evidence requires fresh verification and a newly saved review, not automatic rescoring. Partial-week progress retains latest-plan denominator and cutoff. Device timezone changes do not silently shift saved ranges/calendar bases. Narrative/review-only saves do not create targets; legacy review-defined values need explicit latest-source binding and provisional labels.

## Actuals and Progress

Agreed calculable KPIs may use reusable readonly SQL recipes, verified on target and previous weeks before acceptance. [Measured actuals](weekly-kpis.md#measured-actuals) owns refresh triggers, isolation, provenance and failure behavior. Completed Sync, Progress **Refresh** and default trainer refresh recompute current/previous weeks regardless of displayed period; successful current-week commitment saves best-effort refresh that week only. Reads/navigation never execute recipes. Failures preserve stale last values, not zero; reviewed awards stay unchanged while declared rules can supply provisional unreviewed points. For older late imports/corrections, ask for one explicit week within the trainer's 12-week window.

Progress shows achieved/available points and percentage against each week's latest targets. Unknown totals are gaps; zero and explained above100 remain visible. All latest-plan numeric charts open together and join history by stable meaning. **Period** immediately reloads them: Last 1 / 2 / 3 / 6 / 12 months, default 2, without date inputs or Load/Apply. Each chart is focusable with nonvisual exact values, cutoff and qualification. There are no measurement selectors, Week details or raw-value panel. The pane scrolls through the last chart even in short/narrow windows. [Report/Progress details](weekly-kpis.md#report-and-progress-presentation) owns calendar/range and rendering rules.

KPIs remain chat-managed. No workouts, background reminders, automatic saves or Garmin write-back are added. Weekly artifacts, KPIs and General Plan survive chat/Profile/Garmin deletion; preserved Markdown is source, not another current value store. Legacy pending proposals are neither model-visible nor automatically applied. [Storage/migration](weekly-kpis.md#storage-migration-and-recovery) requires explicit backed-up activation; source delivery never upgrades or restarts a running database.

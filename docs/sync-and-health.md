# Sync, history import and Health views

How explicit Sync, historical import, daily-health finality and the Health views behave. Connection and key handling are in [Garmin connection](garmin-auth.md); additional Garmin fields are in [additional Garmin data](garmin-extra-data.md).

## Sync latest and historical import

**Sync latest** refreshes recent health/activity/body facts and baseline/detail supplements without advancing or resetting saved historical exploration. **Import older data** starts explicit exploration within the current automatic or selected scope; **Continue historical import** appears only for verified executable saved work. A history record or unknown remote boundary alone is not backlog. Completed selected ranges stay completed after repeat refresh/reload. Processed activity records and refreshed health dates are upsert counts, not proven new records; stored totals remain separate.

One explicit Sync drains all eligible known activity details, including activities admitted later during historical import. There is no per-run detail-count quota or default selection ceiling. Available/partial/unsupported detail is terminal, not repeatedly fetched. Failed attempts stop safely rather than retrying in a loop; attempted, completed and remaining known details are distinct. The visible current phase and incomplete reason/next action remain outside Technical details. Latest/history results and reload share additive `garmin_sync_v1` operation truth; legacy absence is unknown, not proof of exhaustion. Cancellation and safety stops retain their actual incomplete status in `sync_run`, not completed. An internal pre-dispatch batch boundary continues automatically within the same claim; in-flight timeouts and cumulative limits do not reset.

Historical import refreshes recent facts once and advances fair health/activity/body/repair batches in one foreground operation, up to **30 minutes or 480 cumulative HTTP requests**, with at least one second between serial dispatches. Each internal batch retains **96 requests / 180 seconds**, each request **30 seconds**. Daily health is read in documented date ranges (31 days for sleep/body battery, up to a year for summaries/HRV), so one range read covers many dates; the counts are actual Garmin HTTP requests, not a proven service allowance. Startup, nested reads and pacing share the non-resetting operation clock. The trainer tool and bodyless legacy HTTP callers retain their existing bounded semantics but refuse saved progress whose scope differs; the dashboard always sends an explicit latest/history/discover intent. Concise results and next work stay visible; **Syncing…** and a decorative spinner identify local or currently confirmed server activity (static cue with reduced motion). Stale metadata and interrupted saved outcomes are not live activity. Collapsed **Technical details** holds run-specific sanitized logs and cursor/count facts, not standing policy paragraphs; older-history attention stays visible independently of latest success.

Discovery reads year-long daily-summary ranges (up to 364 days per request, never below a selected limit) and prioritizes dates with wellness data. Under a selected date limit, empty dates never skip reads. Under **automatic discovery**, each historical batch first probes the next older year; when a full year has no wellness data, discovery stops at the oldest date with data (`noDataBefore`) and Sync shows **Older history complete — No Garmin data found before <date>** with no Continue button, instead of checking empty days one by one. This is a practical stop for the connected account's daily summaries, not a certified account inception; data that only exists before a whole empty year is not explored automatically. Family cursors remain independent. **Cancel sync**, disconnect or app exit stops dispatch and owned work, retaining committed data. Restart and safety pauses require explicit **Continue historical import** when saved work is actionable; setup Finish, launch, navigation and summary reads never start discovery. Authentication/rate/protocol/storage failures and no safe progress stop automatic admission without retries. Refresh summary after cancellation/lost response before another action; failed/stale summary blocks replay. A live server claim still refuses overlap. Persisted running without a live claim is interrupted, not a permanent lock.

The durable summary separates checked older dates, dates with metrics, oldest imported date and unresolved work. Empty dates and activity exhaustion never prove earliest remote health: that boundary remains **unknown**. Existing valid saved dates remain optional advanced exploration limits (not coverage proof); clear the field and explicitly relaunch to select automatic discovery. Old global forward checkpoints are retained but never interpreted as reverse cursors. Family progress remains in `garmin_history_v1`; separate additive `garmin_discovery_v1` stores minimized hint provenance, queued candidate dates and historical operation phase/attempts/batches/stop reason. Additive `garmin_latest_v1` retains latest-refresh settlement/counters independently; summary reads never write checkpoints. When settings switch from a saved date limit to automatic discovery, valid settled or paused limited progress offers one explicit **Discover older history** action: it keeps family cursors, counters, unresolved work and imported data, commits the history/discovery scope change atomically under the Sync claim and continues older than the old limit (a frozen activity window keeps its original lower bound until it finishes; a steps hint that already passed the old limit is re-read once from just below it). Summary reads, launch and Sync latest never transition. Other date-limit changes, disagreeing/one-sided checkpoints and failed/no-progress history show a short specific reason without an executable recovery action. Unsupported/corrupt checkpoints refuse rather than reset. Existing normalized records and date-keyed weight history are preserved. A source rollback can ignore the new checkpoint but must preserve DB/backups; the older exhaustive importer has its own behavior and is not a bounded-sync substitute.

**Sync latest health finality** is independent of metric completeness and uses Garmin's own daily coverage. An ended desktop-local calendar day becomes operationally final only when its daily summary reports wellness coverage for the whole local day (its local start at midnight, its end at the next midnight, consistent GMT offsets including 23/25-hour DST days) after that day has ended and its normalized download succeeded. Partial, malformed or missing coverage keeps the day mutable; today stays mutable. Coverage is evidence that Garmin holds the whole day, never proof of a particular watch upload. Final complete, partial and recognized source-empty days are skipped across restarts; missing fields remain missing. Non-final dates and the complete forward tail remain eligible without a 14-day cutoff, within the exploration floor and normal operation limits. The first official-reader run over data imported by the earlier connector rechecks the most recent 14 local dates (and any non-final ones), keeps older upload-based finals as legacy-final and leaves other data untouched. Refreshes preserve prior non-null metrics and valid zero/downward corrections. Coverage finality is operational, not a promise that Garmin never revises older cloud records.

Latest has no artificial dispatch spacing; serial cancellation, limits and fatal auth/rate handling remain. The single recent-activity request still covers approximately 14 UTC dates, and all required details still drain; activity details and exact HR time in zones come from each activity's original file (in-process FIT decode), falling back to the activity list's zone buckets or, with boundaries in the file, a labelled estimate. Profile/heart-rate-zone attempts occur at most once per local day across restart, including failed attempts and the body-profile path. FIT baseline runs only when the newest known activity differs from the last successful applicable FIT activity; no activity skips it. Skipped/failed sources preserve usable baseline facts. Additive `garmin_latest_freshness_v1` stores these rules without a schema upgrade or history/discovery writes. A settled same-day repeat normally needs about nine HTTP requests (one key exchange, one range read per daily source, activity list, weight and body history) and downloads no files.

## Health views

Health **Daily** compares seven consecutive calendar dates as individual source
values, not a weekly aggregate. Previous/Next step seven dates (including gaps);
Latest returns to the newest imported date at or before today. The range survives
tabs, away/back and overlapping post-sync refreshes. Missing records/fields and
outside-loaded history are explicit, and today is marked in progress on the UTC
sync calendar. Responsive metric charts retain all seven date slots, with exact
source values available by pointing to, focusing or tapping the date buttons.
Calories combines resting (below) and active in one stack with a shared exact
breakdown and independent recorded total. Incomplete breakdowns omit the stack;
known values remain available, and differing component sums are explicit.
Sleep stages combine REM (bottom), Deep and Light (top) in one absolute-duration
stacked-bar card in Daily, Weekly and Monthly, with a shared exact readout that
shows the independently recorded sleep duration. The order is a display choice,
not a ranking of sleep quality. Weekly/Monthly show each stage's existing daily
average over its own available days. An incomplete breakdown omits the stack,
known values stay readable and a differing stage sum is stated, never reconciled;
Awake, Nap and sleep score remain separate charts.
Collapsed per-date details preserve source coverage and categorical HRV context;
gaps are not interpolated or filled with zero. Weekly/Monthly calculations and their bounded imported input remain unchanged.
